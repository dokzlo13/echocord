## Implementation contract

`outputLevelDbfs` is a nullable scalar peak over actual complete stereo frames handed to the native reader after gain/mute, before Discord. Hold a peak for at most 200 ms; silence/starvation and expired observations use -96 dBFS. Initial/opening/stopped/missing/error states are null. Running mute immediately reports the silence floor; unmute requires a handoff in the new mute generation. Gain changes require an observation matching the current settings. Capture `levelDbfs` stays pre-gain and independent of mute. Both fields are finite/clamped at the protocol boundary.

The generated contract was delivered separately for concurrent frontend implementation. The coordinator owns integration and completion.
