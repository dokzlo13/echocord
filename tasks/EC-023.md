## Native design and implementation checkpoint

The independently reviewed implementation uses a persistent tokenless `--audio-worker` and the existing disposable Discord `--engine-worker`. Both are parent-owned Windows jobs. The parent creates an unnamed pagefile-backed mapping but never maps PCM; only narrowly inheritable duplicate handles enter the explicit child handle lists. One fixed 1,920-frame stereo ring uses aligned atomic sample pairs and per-slot sequence checks. Each destination attachment begins at the current cursor; stale producer heartbeat yields paced silence. No filesystem audio transport, frontend samples or PCM-in-JSON were introduced.

The audio worker owns a common 5 ms / 240-frame timeline and nonblocking drains of up to eight source queues. Samples sum at unity and clamp to −1…1 after the sum; there is no automatic normalization or drift resampler. Native pre-gain source clipping is latched, source peaks are measured after precise fractional-dB gain and before mute, and mix overload is observed before clamp. Interval peaks/clip flags are accumulated over the bounded telemetry interval. The destination meter remains the actual post-volume/mute reader handoff, not listener receipt. Unavailable gateway latency stays null.

Opening and stopping are asynchronous per physical source owner. Generation/revision fencing prevents stale lifecycle results from overwriting newer intent. Retiring and opening owners retain their capacity and overlap reservations until cleanup; new requests wait for released capacity. Native release/open deadlines or explicitly unconfirmed cleanup terminate the audio worker, whose parent confirms exit or exposes cleanup failure. Ordinary source faults stay on their card and the stable mix reader/network session remain alive. Duplicate/ancestor-descendant app roots are rejected; app identities retain creation-time validation.

Version-2 preferences migrate recording selection, destination and output without changing credentials. Restored recording cards capture when Restore setup is enabled; restored app cards stay stopped and explicit Capture validates the exact saved identity. Restarted apps require selection. Restored rooms stay idle. Future-version preference files are preserved. Window geometry is a separate shell sidecar. Startup activation occurs only after the desktop single-instance gate, preventing a rejected second launch from restoring captures.

Card commands are immediate. Room changes safely dispose and reconnect the destination, including same-server changes; seamless room moves are not claimed. Source edits never request bot leave. Destination count remains one; independent bus readers provide a later fan-out seam.
